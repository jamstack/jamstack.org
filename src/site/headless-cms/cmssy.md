## About Cmssy

Cmssy is a hosted, headless-only CMS: it serves the admin and the content API, and your own app renders the site. Page sections are defined in your code and edited visually in the admin.

- [Get started](https://www.cmssy.com/docs/start)
- [Live demos](https://www.cmssy.com/demos) - the same content on Next.js, Astro and React Router
- [Examples on GitHub](https://github.com/cmssy-io/examples)

## Key features

- **Content** - pages, data models and records, media, forms, i18n, draft preview, version history and editorial workflow.
- **Commerce** - products with variants and inventory, carts, checkout, orders, discounts, refunds, fulfillment and customer accounts. Payments stay with your provider; Cmssy records the outcome.
- **APIs** - GraphQL delivery API, webhooks, CLI and TypeScript types.
- **AI agents** - an [MCP server](https://www.cmssy.com/docs/api/mcp) with 90+ tools and an in-admin assistant (bring your own key). Refunds, cancellations and page deletes wait for your confirmation.

## Pricing

Free Hobby plan (1 workspace, 25 pages). Pro adds cart and checkout, 5 workspaces and 5 seats for $15/month ($12.50/month billed annually). Enterprise pricing is custom. See [pricing](https://www.cmssy.com/pricing).
