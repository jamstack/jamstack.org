## Edit content right on your site

Sitecog is a hosted headless CMS for websites built on any stack: plain HTML, WordPress, React, Next.js, Vue and others. Developers keep their own code and hosting; editors change texts and images in place, directly on the page, without learning a separate admin UI.

- **On-page (visual) editing**: editors click a block on the live page and change it in place.
- **REST content API**: fetch content at build time from any static site generator or at runtime from any framework.
- **Auto-translation**: translate site content into up to 20 languages.

## More than content

Sitecog also covers the tools a small business website usually needs next to the CMS:

- **Built-in CRM** for leads and contacts
- **Live chat** with Telegram alerts
- **Lead forms without a backend** — submissions go straight to the CRM
- **Traffic analytics and funnels**
- **Click and scroll heatmaps**

All data is stored in the EU.
