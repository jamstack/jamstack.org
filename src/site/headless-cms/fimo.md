## Fimo

Fimo is an AI website platform and CMS for sites built in code. Your application stays code; its content is stored and edited in Fimo, and Fimo hosts the previews and the live site.

### Schema-driven content

Define content types as JSON schemas: a Collection for repeated entries, such as posts, products or team members, and a Single for one set of content, such as a homepage or site settings. `fimo schemas push` generates a typed client for your framework. In Studio, editors change text and images on the page, edit other fields in Content, manage media in the media library and review form submissions.

### A Branch and a preview for every change

Each change is made on its own Branch, which isolates code, content, assets and preview. `fimo deploy` creates a hosted Preview to review, and the live site changes only when someone publishes.

### Built-in agents

SEO, GEO (AI visibility), Content and Quality agents keep improving the site after launch, on a schedule or when the site changes. They publish their changes on their own, or wait for approval if the team asks them to, and every version is kept so any of them can be restored.

### Built for coding agents

`npx fimo@latest init` creates a new site or connects an existing application. Coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Cursor drive Fimo through the `fimo` CLI and a hosted MCP server.

### Pricing

Fimo is free to start: the Free plan costs nothing and needs no credit card. Paid plans are listed on the [pricing page](https://fimo.ai/pricing).
